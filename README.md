# Coda cost calculator

What it costs to run a digital memorial service, and what to charge for it.

Move four sliders (how many memorials, photos in each, how often they are opened,
what you charge) and pick which photo size visitors receive. The page works out the
monthly bill, the cost of one memorial over a century, who gets paid, and which
price points cover a hundred years of storage.

Rates were checked on 17 August 2026 against Google Cloud and Vercel pricing pages.
Photo sizes and visitor behaviour are estimates.

Live at https://poojan-coda.github.io/costCalc/

No build step, no dependencies. `index.html` is the whole thing.
